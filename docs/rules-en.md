# Manillen with 2 players — rules (English, as implemented)

Translated from `rules.md` and corrected after clarification with the author.
Where this file and `rules.md` differ, **this file is authoritative**.

## Deck and card order

32 cards (piquet deck): 7, 8, 9, J, Q, K, A, 10 in clubs, diamonds, hearts, spades.
Rank order within a suit, low → high: **7 8 9 J Q K A 10**. The 10 ("Manille") is the highest card.

| Card | Points |
|------|--------|
| 10 (Manille) | 5 |
| A | 4 |
| K | 3 |
| Q | 2 |
| J | 1 |
| 9, 8, 7 | 0 |

60 points per deal in total.

## Dealing

1. 4 cards face down in a row for the non-dealer, then 4 face down for the dealer.
   These are unknown to **everyone**, including their owner.
2. 4 cards face up on top of each player's face-down cards (public).
3. The remaining 16 cards are dealt as hands: 8 to the non-dealer, 8 to the dealer (private).

Each player: 8 hand cards + 4 face-up table cards + 4 face-down table cards.

## Bidding

- The dealer **must** choose: a trump suit, or **no trump** ("muulen", score ×2). No passing.
- The non-dealer may then **contra** ("meegaan"): score ×2 again. No-trump + contra = ×4.
- Multipliers apply to whoever wins the deal.

## Play

- The non-dealer leads the first trick.
- A trick is **one card per player** (2 cards). 16 tricks per deal.
- The leader may play any hand card or any face-up table card.
- When a face-up table card is played, the face-down card beneath it is turned face up at once and is available from the next trick on.
- The follower must:
  1. follow suit; if holding a card of the led suit that beats the led card, a beating card **must** be played;
  2. if void of the led suit: play a trump if holding any (trump deals);
  3. otherwise: any card.
  If trump is led, rule 1 applies to trumps (a lower trump is forced only when no higher trump is held).
- Trick winner: the higher trump if any trump was played, else the higher card of the led suit. The winner leads next.
- Players may look at the **last trick only**. Earlier tricks must be remembered.

## Scoring

- Each player sums the points in the tricks they won.
- The winner scores (points − 30) × multiplier. 30–30 scores 0 for both.
- Match: deals alternate the dealer; first to the target (default 101) wins.
- No last-trick bonus (assumption; not in the original rules).
